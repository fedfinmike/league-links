How a player link is counted

Two players are linked only when both are recorded for the same team in the same match. Being in the same squad or on the same club list does not count as a game together.

What we are adding next

NSW Cup and Queensland Cup come first, followed by NYC and Jersey Flegg, SG Ball, Harold Matthews where it is under-17 or older, Mal Meninga, Cyril Connell from its under-17 era, Queensland Colts and other representative teams.
